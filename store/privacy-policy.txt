Privacy Policy — سەڵاتی (Sallaty)

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Sallaty (سەڵاتی) is a prayer-times, Qur'an and adhkar app. This policy explains
what the app does and does not do with your information.

کورتەی کوردی

سەڵاتی هیچ زانیارییەکی کەسیت کۆناکاتەوە و بۆ هیچ شوێنێک نانێرێت.

- هیچ هەژمارێک (account) پێویست نییە.
- هیچ سێرڤەرێکی ئێمە نییە — هەموو زانیارییەکان تەنها لەناو مۆبایلەکەی خۆتدا دەمێننەوە.
- شوێنەکەت (GPS) تەنها لەناو مۆبایلەکەدا بەکاردێت بۆ دیاریکردنی کاتی نوێژ و ئاراستەی قیبلە — بۆ دەرەوە نانێردرێت.
- ئەگەر ئەپەکە بسڕیتەوە، هەموو زانیارییەکان لەگەڵیدا دەسڕێنەوە.
- ئەپەکە هیچ ڕیکلامێک پیشان نادات و هیچ ناسنامەیەکی ڕیکلامی ئامێرەکەت
  بەکارناهێنێت.

ملخّص بالعربية

تطبيق سَلاتي لا يجمع أي بيانات شخصية ولا يرسلها إلى أي جهة.

- لا يتطلّب أي حساب.
- لا نملك أي خادم — تبقى جميع البيانات على هاتفك فقط.
- يُستخدم موقعك (GPS) داخل الهاتف فقط لتحديد أوقات الصلاة واتجاه القبلة، ولا يُرسل إلى الخارج.
- عند حذف التطبيق تُحذف جميع البيانات معه.
- لا يعرض التطبيق أي إعلانات، ولا يستخدم معرّف الإعلان الخاص بجهازك.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. Sallaty has no user accounts, no
analytics, no advertising, and no backend server operated by us. We cannot see
who you are or how you use the app.

2. Information stored on your device

The following stays only on your device (in the app's local storage) and is
never transmitted to us:

- Chosen city / last known coordinates — Calculating prayer times and qibla direction
- Language, theme, adhan sound, notification settings — Remembering your preferences
- Qur'an bookmarks and last-read position — Resuming your reading
- Dhikr counters and congregation-prayer log — Your personal tracking

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

3. Location

If you grant location permission, the app reads your approximate coordinates to
pick the nearest city's official prayer times and to compute the qibla bearing.
This happens entirely on the device. Location is never uploaded to us or
shared with third parties. You may refuse the permission and instead choose your
city manually — the app works fully either way.

4. Notifications and alarms

The app schedules local alarms so the adhan sounds at each prayer time, including
when the screen is off. These are local device alarms; no push service and no
network connection is involved.

5. Network use

The app is designed to work fully offline. Prayer times for the supported
cities, the Qur'an text, translations and adhan audio are all bundled inside the
app. The app may optionally contact the public website amozhgary.tv to refresh
official prayer times for a new year. This request contains no personal data.
If you open "nearby mosques", the app hands the request to Google Maps, which is
governed by Google's own privacy policy.

6. Advertising

Sallaty shows no adverts.

Earlier versions carried a single banner supplied by Google AdMob. It was
removed at the request of the people who use the app, who felt that taking
advertising revenue from a religious app was not right. The AdMob SDK is no
longer bundled, the app never reads your device's advertising identifier, and
no advertising network receives anything from it.

We use no analytics and no other tracking SDKs either. Sallaty has no revenue
model: it is given away.

7. Children

The app is rated for ages 13 and over on Google Play. It is not directed at
children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
