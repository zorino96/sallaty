```
Sallaty is your daily prayer companion — completely offline, with no account and no tracking.

PRAYER TIMES YOU CAN TRUST
Official prayer times for more than 50 cities across Kurdistan and Iraq, taken from the published tables rather than estimated on the device. Choose your city by hand — useful when travelling — or let the app pick the nearest one from your location.

A REAL ADHAN, EVEN WHEN LOCKED
The adhan sounds at every prayer time and wakes the screen like an alarm clock, so you hear it with the phone in your pocket. Three recitations to choose from, or a silent notification if you prefer.

THE QUR'AN, IN KURDISH
The full Uthmani text with a Kurdish translation, verse search, bookmarks and your last-read position remembered.

QIBLA COMPASS
Points to the Kaaba using the phone's compass, with the bearing shown in degrees.

ADHKAR AND DHIKR
Morning and evening adhkar, the adhkar after prayer, and a tap counter for dhikr.

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
No account. No servers of ours. Nothing you do in the app leaves your phone, and deleting the app deletes everything with it. The Qur'an text, the translations, the prayer times and the adhan recordings are all bundled inside the app, so it works with no connection at all.

A small banner advert sits at the bottom of the screen, below the navigation bar. It is always a banner — never a full-screen or interrupting format, and it never covers what you are reading. No advert is shown while the adhan is sounding.

CONTENT SOURCES
Prayer times: amozhgary.tv
Kurdish translation: Tafsiri Asan — Burhan Muhammad-Amin
Uthmani script: Tanzil.net
Adhan recordings: Aaqib Azeez (CC BY-SA 4.0), Sabah Fakhri (Creative Commons), Fouad Adan (Public Domain)
```

## Age rating — 4+ (decided)

The Play listing declares 13+; Apple computes its rating from a content
questionnaire instead, and on content alone Sallaty answers "none" to every
question, which yields **4+**. That is the honest answer for the content, and
it matches how most prayer apps are rated.

The two stores therefore disagree, on purpose, because they are measuring
different things: Play's 13+ follows from serving third-party adverts, Apple's
4+ follows from the content itself. The adverts are still constrained to
G-rated inventory with 12 sensitive categories blocked at the AdMob account
level, so 4+ does not weaken anything a user actually sees.

Note this is *not* the same as the "Made for Kids" category — answer **No** to
that; the app is not directed at children.
